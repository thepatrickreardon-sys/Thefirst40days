Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

The First 40 Days is run by [your name or business], [city, state]. This policy explains what we collect when you use the app and what we do with it. In short: we collect only what's needed to run your family's page, we never sell it, and you can delete it any time.

What we collect

  • Families who start a page: your email address and sign-in details, and what you put on your page: names, the due date or birthday, your address if you add it, food allergy notes, visit notes and your requests for help.
  • Helpers: the name you sign up with, the times you sign up for, any notes you write, and your phone number if you choose to give it. Helpers don't need an account.
  • On your device: the app keeps a few small settings in your browser (like your name and the pages you've opened) so you don't have to retype them, plus an anonymous ID so it remembers your sign-ups.

We don't use advertising trackers or sell your information to anyone.

Who can see it

A family's page can be seen by anyone who has its private link. That includes the schedule, helper names and the address the family adds, so families should only share the link with people they trust. Helper phone numbers are visible only to that helper and the family who runs the page. Co-manager email addresses are visible only to the family.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase, which processes it on our behalf. Sign-in with Google or Apple is handled by those companies under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep a family's page until the family deletes it or asks us to. Sign-ups stay on a page until the helper or the family removes them.

Deleting your information

Families can delete their page at any time from the Manage tab, which erases every request, sign-up and phone number on it. Anyone can delete their account, or remove their own sign-ups and phone number, from the My Shifts tab. You can also email us at [support email] and we'll do it for you.

Children

The app is meant for adults. Families may add a child's first name or routine to their page; please share only what helpers need to know.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we'll post the new version here with a new date. Questions: [support email].
