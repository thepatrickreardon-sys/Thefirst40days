Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

The First 40 Days ("we") is run by [business name], [city, state]. This policy explains what we collect when you use the app and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Families who start a page: your email address and sign-in details, and what you put on your page: names, the due date or birthday, your address if you add it, allergy notes, visit notes and your requests for help.
  • Helpers: the name you sign up with, the times you sign up for, any notes, and your phone number if you choose to give it.
  • Technical data: an anonymous account ID so the app remembers your sign-ups on your device.

Who can see it

A family's page can be seen by anyone who has its private link. That includes the schedule, helper names and the address the family adds. Helper phone numbers are visible only to that helper and the family who runs the page. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase in the United States. Google processes it on our behalf.

Deleting your information

Families can delete their page at any time from the Manage tab, which erases every request, sign-up and phone number on it. Anyone can delete their account, or remove their own sign-ups and phone number, from the My Shifts tab. You can also email us at [support email].

Children

The app is for adults. Families may add a child's first name or routine to their page; please share only what helpers need.

Changes and contact

We'll post any changes here. Questions: [support email].
